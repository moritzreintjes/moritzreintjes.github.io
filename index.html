Moritz Reintjes

Assistant Professor

Geometric Analysis · General Relativity · Nonlinear PDE

I work on singular structures in Lorentzian geometry, relativistic fluid dynamics, and mathematical quantum physics. A central theme of my research is the analysis and regularization of singular geometries by the RT-equations.

Research program Publications Curriculum vitae
Research program

Singular geometries

Removability of singularities, the RT-equations (elliptic regularization), essential regularity, Uhlenbeck compactness, cosmic censorship, low-regularity singularity theorems.

Relativistic Fluid Dynamics

Shock waves, relativistic dissipation, stellar phase transitions, conservation laws, gravitational collapse.

Mathematical Quantum Physics

Causal Fermion Systems, quantum states and dynamics, the fermionic signature operator.
